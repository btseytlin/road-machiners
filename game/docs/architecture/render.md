# Rendering and UI

- `src/three/render/vehicle.ts` draws a flat silhouette of a seen vehicle where terrain or props cover it. Truck meshes mark stencil bit `TRUCK_BIT` and props mark `PROP_BIT`, so other views must not write those bits.
- `src/three/pointer.ts` decides what a click on the map does. The player truck is picked by its drawn shape, through a ray hit on its model, and the same decision drives the stop cursor and the ground highlight. NPC picking keeps the screen radius.
- `VehicleView.aim()` turns each turret only within the `fireSpans()` of its gun, through `aimWithin()` in `src/sim/armor.ts`, so a barrel shows where the gun can fire. A target outside the arc leaves the barrel at the nearest edge. A gun with no target rests forward, or at its nearest allowed edge, and a gun that can fire nowhere rests forward. The head stands clear of its sweep: `VehicleView.rebuild()` draws the body, items and wheels first, then raises each gun's post over every drawn item and body surface the head can turn over, by `GUN_GAP` (`src/three/render/gunClearance.ts`).
- Trucks and every prop in the prop scope get a dark outline 0.5 CSS pixels wide from `outlineOf()` and `outlineProps()` in `src/three/render/models.ts`. It is geometry, back faces pushed out in screen space, so antialiasing smooths it. Outlines draw only on pixels no model has marked in the stencil.
- `src/three/render/truckMotion.ts` leans the drawn truck body and swings its antenna and tow chain from the acceleration in each physics frame. It is render-only, so physics never reads it. Shocks and axles stretch between the leaning body and the physics wheel hubs. The lean holds while a turn is paused.
- `src/three/render/beaconPulse.ts` draws the player's beacon as red full rings every `PULSE.period` seconds of wall-clock time while `player.beacon` is on, under the sound arcs. It keeps a fixed set of meshes and reads no turn state.
- `RadioLights` in `src/three/render/vehicle.ts` plays antenna light cues on wall-clock time, so an open call still glows with frozen turns. The open call comes from `callTrucks()`, short talk from `radioSpeakers()` and the beacon switch-on from `world.player.beacon`. `radioLit()` gives the light from a cue. `VehicleView.radio()` lights the bulb and its halo.
- `src/three/render/scope.ts` detaches map chunks outside the camera view or past gray vision. Gray vision reaches `TERRAIN.vision.grayFactor` sight radii from the truck. Its `SightLimit` clips ground and props at that edge and greys props on tiles out of clear sight. The camera cannot pan past the edge. Static views register with a scope instead of adding to the scene.
- `VehicleLights` in `src/three/render/daylight.ts` gives each lamp-on truck a headlight beam. The four nearest the player truck cast shadows from 1024² maps, so props, trucks and terrain block them. Farther beams light without shadows, to cap the depth passes. Both beam pools only grow through the night and by day hold just the lamps that are on, because a change in the shadowed-light count recompiles every material.
- Road shoulders are drawn twice. The road shader in `src/three/render/roads.ts` frays the edge both ways where the blurred road mask lies between `SHOULDER_FROM` and the road edge: the road dither keeps road pixels out on the sand, light rim sand `PAL.roadRim` and sparse grey stones `PAL.stoneGrey`, more of each nearer the road. The fray scales with the desert weight of the ground beside the road. Each road tile takes the look of the nearest ground off the road, from `lookTypes()` in `src/render/groundPaint.ts`, and the ground paint, the road shader and the scatter all read it. So roads beside salt, mud, asphalt, fields and other zero-weight ground keep the plain edge, the plain shoulder and the base scatter. `scatterPlacements()` in `src/three/render/scatter.ts` raises the stone chance on desert tiles within `SHOULDER_TILES` past `ROAD_GAP` and keeps scrub and cacti off them. Open desert moves toward denser desert stones, scrub and cacti by `desertWeight()`, and cacti gather within `CACTUS_NEAR_ROCK` of rocks and crags. Ground with no desert weight keeps the sparse small pebbles and dry scrub, shoulders included. Scatter stays decoration without collision.
- Props collide, block routes and block sight by their model's shape. `npm run models:shapes` turns each prop model into boxes in `src/data/prop-shapes.json`, and a test fails when a shape is stale. `propPose()` in `src/sim/mapgen.ts` is the one turn and scale of each prop, read by the views and every collision user. `propBoxes()` gives its posed boxes and `propReach()` the circle that holds them, for cheap tests. Physics gives colliders only to props in one turn's reach of a truck, and leaves out boxes above `PHYSICS.truckClearance`. Sight is blocked only by boxes that span eye height.
- `src/ui/log.ts` owns the event log. `LogBook` keeps 200 lines stamped with the HUD clock (`clockLabel()`), and `LogPanel` shows them newest first and keeps the reader's scroll place when lines arrive. The panel is a flex column, so the lines fill the room below the header and scroll inside it, and the panel's own height is the only height rule. It expands from its header button and shrinks back while a modal is open.
- A truck fast enough breaks fences, junk and dead trees, which grow back after days. At the physics step of the hit, `src/three/render/debris.ts` splits the prop's model into its separate parts, which fly in a render-only Rapier world, get shoved by the trucks and freeze where they come to rest. A prop broken out of view or before a load topples over at once.
- Map rocks and dead trees are drawn as one instanced model per terrain chunk, built at the first sync of `src/three/render/obstacles.ts`. `TreeInstances` in `src/three/render/trees.ts` keeps each tree's chunk and instance index. A broken tree's instance gets a zero matrix, which hides it and its outline, and gets its standing matrix back when the tree grows back. A rock or tree id the bake did not place throws.
- Trucks draw part wear in `WEAR_LOOK_STEPS` look steps from HP (`wearLookStep()` in `src/render/partLooks.ts`). Each step fades the part toward one dusty gray (`WORN_GRAY`) and jags its vertices, thin models less (`JAG_THIN`). The cab part wears the base body and bumpers. The truck rebuilds only when a part crosses a step, so a repair restores the look. A part that a hit breaks throws `good_scrap` pieces from its drawn spot through `PartDebris` in `src/three/render/partDebris.ts`, which shares the flight code of prop debris. `breakSignature()` adds an ammo blast for a weapon, an air cloud for a wheel and a fire burst for a fuel tank or jerrycans. The break plays as the shown round that dealt the part's last damage lands (`src/three/breakCues.ts` pairs each `partDisabled` with that round), with the `part-broken` sound, and the part takes its broken look in the same frame. A break with no shown round, such as a collision's, plays at the end of the shot band. The pieces fade away after a while and the live pieces are capped. Both are render only: a load shows the wear but plays no break effects.
- `HazardViews` in `src/three/render/hazards.ts` draws smoke clouds, ground fields, flares, emitter pulses and the sparks on shut-down trucks, as the things they are. No hazard view draws a ring, disc or band at a hazard's radius. Smoke is a dense plume of puffs that thins over the player's truck (`cutShare()`), an oil slick is opaque glossy blobs with noise-shaped rims that merge into one streak, caltrops are spikes strewn along the drop line, and a pulse is a flash with jagged arcs to the trucks it hit. `src/three/render/lines.ts` draws harpoon ropes between the two part points, straight even when slack.
- Hazards made in the turn now playing show when they happen, from the `TurnClock` that `game.ts` passes: the playback's world before the turn, its movement progress and whether movement is done. A field shows once its dropper's animated spot has passed it (`fieldShown()`). Smoke, flares, harpoon shots and pulses start at the volley (`volleyShown()`). A turn with no gunfire ends its playback in the frame its movement ends, so `HazardViews` keeps that playback's world before the turn until the next turn and meets the volley there. Hazards from earlier turns and after a load show at once. The sim keeps its order; this is render timing only.
- A `Flight` arc carries a flare from its cannon's `partPoint` to its hang point, where it lights, and a mortar shell to the point where its cloud then billows. A launcher the player cannot see starts it at its last contact point, else at the target. A new Sprout cloud billows out from its truck. A harpoon shot grows its rope from the harpoon to its anchor. The claymore blast goes through the volley blast effect in `src/three/volley.ts`.
- `src/ui/weapons.ts` owns the utility row, its slot states and the number keys of every slot: guns first, then utilities (`pressKey`, `utilityKey`). `src/three/utility-aim.ts` turns the clicks of a selected point utility into orders and draws its reach band, the effect outline under the pointer and a small cross on a set point. The rules stay in `src/sim/utility.ts`.
- The harpoon's rope: `Projectiles` in `src/three/render/projectiles.ts` trails it behind the bolt in flight. On a hit it holds it until the playback ends, and on a miss it lets it lie, then reels it in. `HarpoonLinesView` in `src/three/render/lines.ts` hides a line shot this turn until its playback ends, then draws it between the two trucks, and reels in the rope of any line that ends.
- Item icons and chassis portraits are cells of sprite sheets that `npm run icons` renders from the models at build time, so no screen runs a 3D render per card. `createItemIcon()`, `itemIconEl()` and `chassisPortrait()` in `src/ui/cards.ts` draw them. [Art pipeline](../art.md#icons) has the pipeline.
- `src/ui/radio.ts` is Waste Of Time Radio. `RadioStation` decides J.J.'s broadcasts from the worlds `Hud.pushEvents()` hears, reading world state and events and never writing them, so no save holds the radio. `RadioPanel` streams each broadcast onto its pager screen in real time, so fast automatic turns do not speed the typing up. Lines and pacing live in `src/data/radio.ts`, and her voice in [Lore](../lore.md#waste-of-time-radio). The sound knobs and mute switch from `src/ui/sound.ts` sit on its faceplate. A raider `hostile` event is a robbery report only when `robbing()` from `src/sim/states.ts` holds, since drivers fighting back raise hostile events too.
- `.right-dock`, made by `rightDock()` in `src/ui/dom.ts`, is the one owner of the right column above the log. It sits at the contracts panel's old spot and stacks the contracts at the bottom and the radio above them, so panels in it carry no position numbers. The hover panel wins the column: `Hud.keepRadioClear` gives the radio the `away` class while the shown hover panel overlaps it, checked on a `ResizeObserver` of both and on window resize, never per frame.
- A kill wreck with a `hulk` draws as its chassis base model through `buildHulk()` in `src/three/render/obstacles.ts`: fully gray, darkened by `HULK_TONE`, jagged at the last wear step and tilted up to `HULK_TILT`, with no wheels, parts, paint or light. Trucks and hulks share the vertex jag `jag()` in `src/three/render/vehicle.ts`. Map, road and convoy wrecks keep the generic `wreck` model.
- An exploding round that bursts on open ground digs a crater (`src/sim/craters.ts`, saved in `World.craters`). The ground heights never change. Physics adds a ring of capsule colliders on the crater's rim near the player, but never under a truck (`syncCraters()` in `src/phys/drive.ts`). `CraterViews` in `src/three/render/craters.ts` draws a scorched disc and a rim at the same points, in its own scope with no outline. A crater dug this turn shows when its blast lands at the shot's `burst` point. Craters fade after `CRATER.days`, out of sight.
- Guns throw casings and seen trucks leave ruts off-road. Both are render only and unsaved. `Casings` in `src/three/render/projectiles.ts` keeps a capped pool and ejects one casing at each round's fire cue, or one per shotgun shell, by `PROJECTILES[...].casing`. `Ruts` in `src/three/render/ruts.ts` lays a strip piece per rear tire (`RUT.wheels`, so one left and one right track) every `RUT.step` of travel while the wheel touches the ground (`WheelFrame.ground`). Ground darkness comes from `TERRAIN_TYPES[...].rut`. Both fade over one in-game day.
- `maxSpeedSteps()` in `src/sim/stats.ts` is the one max-speed rule. `speedRows()` in `hud-readout.ts` words it as one terse line per step that moves the number, like `Load: −10 km/h`, and `MaxSpeedView` in `hud.ts` shows it in a tooltip that CSS opens on hover and focus. `Hud.renderTop()` keeps persistent slots and never detaches that node, so focus and hover survive the per-frame refresh.

- `src/ui/tips.ts` shows first-time tips for driving and the horn, one at a time. A tip goes away for good when the player closes it or does what it says. Seen tips live in browser storage apart from the save. New game clears them. A new game first shows the opening tips. `openingStep()` derives the step from saved facts about the opening wreck of `src/sim/opening.ts`, the engine and the cage, so a reload resumes it and an old save has none. While the step's tip is unseen, no other tip shows or counts as done, so the driving tips start whole after the cage is mounted. An opening tip carries the `opening-tip` class and stays on screen under an open modal, in the hidden weapons panel's place, since it names steps in the inventory. Every other HUD panel hides under a modal. The driving tips count a truck as moving only above `RULES.parkedSpeed`, since a truck parked by physics keeps a tiny speed. Boot logs the start kit's opening line once for a new game, never on load.
- A HUD panel that holds a single control gives the control the whole box, with no padding outside it, as in `.recenter`, `.action`, help, feedback and camera mode. `npm run playtest` fails when a corner of such a panel misses its control.
- `src/ui/game-menu.ts` owns the Menu button in the top right and its dropdown of New Game, Save, Load and Help, with the controls guide in `HelpPanel`. While the dropdown is open it swallows keys and click-aways, and `Game.modalOpen()` counts it. `src/ui/new-game.ts` is the one New game flow: the Menu, the death screen and the broken-save screen all call `chooseNewGame()`, and only a confirmed Start reloads into a new world.
- `src/audio/ plays sound through Web Audio. `src/data/sounds.ts` lists every cue and its files in `public/sfx/`, and a test keeps both in sync. Every file goes through `scripts/sfx-lib.mjs`, which sets loudness per sound group and one format. Generated prompts start with the `SOUND_STYLE` entry for the cue's recording setup, so sounds stay consistent. [Sound](../sound.md) explains the mix, the combat score and how to add, generate and tune sounds.
- Game keys are bare keys. A keydown with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held belongs to the browser, and every game key handler skips it through `isBrowserChord()` in `src/ui/dom.ts`.
- Lit lamps and windows glow in their own color, with no point lights: a `PAL.lamp.on` box in `SiteBuilder` and a model material named `glow` get an emissive equal to their color. Fortress gate lamps, guns and banners stand on the gatehouse's outer face from `fortressGates()`, and `gateGunPoint()` is where both the gun model and a guard shot's tracer start.
- Moving scenery (the Bowl windmill, the Dustwell pumpjack, the Granary conveyor sacks, the Salvage Yard crane and the Nose radar dish) is render-only, so no rule reads it. `SiteBuilder.addMover()` registers a part whose origin is its pivot, and `addSites()` hands every mover to one `SiteMotion` in `src/three/render/site-motion.ts`. It ticks through the prop scope's `onFrame()` hook, once per drawn frame, and it sets each part's local pose on real time from its rest pose. The motions are `spin`, `rock`, `slew`, `hoist` and `travel`, which carries a load along a belt and jumps it back. Each site's time offset comes from `hash2` of its position. `tick()` touches only the registered parts.
- `vehicleLampsOn()` in `src/three/render/daylight.ts` decides whether a vehicle's lamps shine. The player's truck follows `player.headlights`, the L key switch, and every other vehicle follows the clock (`lampsOn()`). `nightLightsWanted()` decides whether the night look exists: the full beam pool and the player glow. It counts only the clock and NPC lamps. By day `VehicleLights` keeps a beam only for each vehicle whose lamps are on, which is the player's, with no glow. Beam strength follows `Daylight.beam`, 1 from sunset on and lower under a high sun. A day toggle adds or removes one light, so it costs one material recompile.

- Sun shadows: `lightScene()` snaps the shadow box to whole texels around the truck, so static shadows hold still while it drives. `src/three/render/shadowFilter.ts` owns the shadow filter, a fixed 3×3 grid of compare taps that replaces three's noise-rotated disk. Heat haze never shifts the shadow term.
