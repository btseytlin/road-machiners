# Map and world

- The map is baked offline, never built in the game. `src/mapgen/bake.ts` runs fixed layers over one draft: base relief, geology, road and site finish, the old world, the new world, territories, ground types and boulders.
- `src/mapgen/newworld.ts` grows scrub from moist ground, fills small basins with dirty water or toxic pools, and places shack camps with fences and junk, and car wrecks, with numbers in `NEW_WORLD`.
- `src/mapgen/oldworld.ts` places ruined settlements, overlook and bend buildings, old asphalt roads, old highways with broken bridges over deep gorges, power lines, billboards, tank hulks and dead fields, with numbers in `OLD_WORLD` in `src/data/terrain.ts`.
- `src/mapgen/geology.ts` holds the rain, slump, wind and dune rules, with numbers in `GEOLOGY` in `src/data/terrain.ts`.
- A territory is a location of kind `territory` in `REGION.locations`: no edge, gates, pads or `site` obstacle, no flattening and no hardpan, so trucks drive in over the natural ground. Its shape is a circle of `radius` round `pos`, or the polygon `outline` when one is set. Old Orchard's outline follows its basin, and the Fallen Sun's runs inside its basin floor and furrow; for an outline, `radius` is its bounding radius, a reach and never an edge. Its rules live in `TERRITORIES` in `src/data/territory.ts`: a seed offset, the spot and debris gaps, the reactor with its hazard, and either a `wreck` or a `farm`. The Fallen Sun is a wreck: authored hull pieces, caches, decks, dirt roads and spurs, and drawn patches, rim rocks and scree. Its piece numbers are measured in the level concept's pixels and its roads in the second reference's, and each comment names them. The furrow, the wing and the flaps are inferred, and their comments say so. Old Orchard is a farm laid out along its old road, the `spine`.
- `siteGap(site, pos)` in `src/sim/sites.ts` owns every location's edge: tiles from pos to the edge, negative inside, from the outline or the circle. `siteEdgeCrossings()` gives where a segment crosses it. Membership, entries, discovery, the old-world clearance, the old-road router, road paint and grading, nav, the territory bake and the farm bake all call these two, and nothing else compares a distance with a location's radius. `groundDiscs()` in `src/render/groundPaint.ts` paints no disc under an outlined location.
- `src/sim/territory.ts` answers every territory question for the sim: membership, spots, entries, hunting grounds and hazard zones. It also turns a wreck's authored layout into map tiles (`territoryPieces`, `territoryCaches`, `territoryRoads`, `landingStrips`, `reactorPos`), so the bake, the render and the tests place things alike. The reactor's position is the hazard's centre. `src/sim/hazard.ts` drains driver health inside a zone, and the nav layer blocks zones for routes only.
- `src/mapgen/territory.ts` fills each territory with its own rng, drawn from the map seed and the offset `TERRITORY_SEED_OFFSET + rules.seed`, so adding a territory shifts no other's draws. For a wreck it seats and places the pieces first, then marks its dirt roads, then places the reactor and the caches, then draws the rim rocks and each patch's field spots and debris. Spots come before debris, so debris never boxes one in. Drawn props keep clear of the pieces' boxes, the dirt roads, the decks, the landing strips past each lip, the roads, the hazard, cliffs, and a farm's old road, pads and tracks.
  - The seat levels the ground under each piece's low boxes to the height at its centre and eases it back to the relief over a few tiles, so a long piece neither floats nor sinks.
  - The ribcage tube and the arch shells are hollow models. Their roofs start above `PHYSICS.truckClearance`, so physics, nav, sight and free-spot checks see only their walls, and trucks drive through with no special case.
  - A wreck's `roads` and `spurs` are `track` roads marked by `markRoads()` in `src/mapgen/marks.ts`, which the farm also calls. A web road stays inside the outline. A spur starts on a web road and runs past the outline into the wasteland, and marking it throws on a cliff, a region road, another site or an earlier prop, so a spur never silently cuts through the map. Boulders keep off every `BUILT_TRACK` tile (`fitsOffRoad()` in `src/mapgen/bake.ts`).
  - `paintRoadMask()` in `src/render/roadPaint.ts` strokes the dirt roads and spurs into the road mask's green channel, a spur fading over its last tiles, and the ground shader in `src/three/render/roads.ts` draws them in `PAL.dirtRoad` with a darker soft shoulder. Region roads stay in the red channel.
- A basin (`TERRAIN.features.basins`, `basin()` in `src/sim/elevation.ts`) is a landform with a polygon floor, a per-vertex bank and rim, a depth and floor swells. The Fallen Sun lies in one: cliff arcs where the bank is short and the rim high, a drivable notch, an east hill, and open banks to the south. It applies before geology, so rain, slump and wind roughen it like any land. Its crash furrow is a channel beside Broken Wing's trench. `paintCraters` skips basins, so no paint marks the floor.
- `fillFarm()` in `src/mapgen/farm.ts` lays out a territory's `FarmRules`. Every position is authored in the road's frame: `onOrchardRoad(s, c)` in `src/data/region.ts` turns tiles `s` along the road toward its north end and `c` across it toward map west into an offset from the centre. `ORCHARD_HEADING` sets the road's heading so it shows at the concept image's 25° screen angle from the fixed camera. Building turns are relative to that heading. The authored skeleton is the roads, pads, canals, building poses, run lines and blocks; everything on it is jittered, broken or scattered from the territory rng. The steps run in order, and each mark takes only a tile no earlier mark took:
  - `roads` are marked in list order: an `oldRoad` as asphalt and a `track` as `BUILT_TRACK` (the `track` ground: hardpan to drive, painted paler so roads show). The first five are the main roads, and each ends within a tile of the outline, so it leaves the farm. The spur road in `REGION` ends at the old road's south end.
  - Pads are marked as `BUILT_PAD` (the `concrete` ground: drives like asphalt, painted pale grey) and `canals` as `BUILT_CANAL` (the `canal` ground: drives like dirty water, painted blue-grey).
  - Each building pose becomes a loot spot, turned by up to its group's `turnJitter` and shifted by up to `shift`. `spotTableAt()` in `src/sim/territory.ts` finds its table by its look, so one look has one table.
  - Runs of segment props (fences, barriers, sandbags) go along their polylines. A `broken` share of segments is dropped and each left segment turns and shifts by its `jitter`. A segment that touches a road, track, canal or pad, reaches into a building or crosses another segment is dropped.
  - Every block marks its field, turned `turn` off the road's frame. Then `clutter` scatters loose pieces round the buildings of the looks each rule names, from the debris gap to `reach` tiles past the building, off roads, marks and fields.
  - Each block gets trees on a grid in its row direction, with jitter, missing points and random turns, then `strays` single trees stand just past the block edges, up to `groves.maxTrees`. Trees keep off the roads, marks, runs, clutter and every building's parking gap.
  - No three run or clutter pieces of one look stand in a ruled row, within 0.1 tile of one line and 0.02 rad of one turn: a run segment that would make one turns a little more or drops, and a clutter draw that would is redrawn.
  - After the farm, `src/mapgen/territory.ts` draws the farm's `debris` in the band along its `spine`, the debris gap from the buildings. The farm draws no field spots: its army caches are authored with its buildings, since its groves leave no open band. Hunting grounds lie in the `grounds` band beside the spine, inside the outline.
- The farm fails loudly on authored parts and drops decoration. A building, pad, road point, canal point, run point or block corner off the outline, on a cliff or on a New World road throws. So does a building on a farm road unless its pose sets `shoulder`, or on a canal. A road's outer end may lie up to a tile past the edge. Trees, strays, run segments and clutter on bad ground are dropped, but a block whose ground rejects more than 30% of the trees it plans (its grid points not left empty) throws. Move an authored position onto good ground and note the old and new `(s, c)` in its comment, never loosen a check.
- Baked props stay out of saves and are rebuilt from the map file on load. `decodeMap()` in `src/sim/terrain.ts` reads the file. The file stores a prop kind as its index in `PROP_KINDS` and a ground type as its index in `TYPE_IDS`. A change to either order, or a removed entry, bumps the file version and needs a re-bake, so a file with the old codes is refused rather than misread. Boot fetches it in `src/three/main.ts`, and tests use `TEST_MAP` from `src/test/map.ts`.
- Broken Wing's hoop is a baked prop, `shipWing`, placed first by `shipWing()` in `oldworld.ts` from `TERRAIN.features.wing`. It is the wing's torn root, bent up and over the road. Its feet block like any ruin. Its boxes over the road start at least `truckClearance` + 1 m up, so physics, nav and sight leave the road under it open like a canopy. `BROKEN_WING` in `src/data/region.ts` places everything at Broken Wing: the straight road (map yaw 0), the hoop, the deck ends, the two sand mounds (`TERRAIN.features.mounds`, bowls with a negative depth, so road grading turns them into ramps), the crash trench (`TERRAIN.features.trench`, a channel) and the site.
- `src/sim/bridge.ts` holds the decks as a list, `DECKS`: Canyon Bridge, the Broken Wing deck and the Fallen Sun's wing and flaps (`FALLEN_SUN_DECKS` in `src/data/territory.ts`). One drivable surface is one deck: a deck's `line` runs through stations on its straight axis, each standing its `rise` over the ground, so the Fallen Sun's wing climbs, runs level and comes down as one deck. `deckSegments()` in `src/sim/terrain.ts` is the only source of a deck's straight pieces and their end heights, which slope, physics, models and pick quads follow. `buildDecks()` throws on decks whose outlines touch, so a surface is never split into decks whose seams every reader must find. A raised end is a lip: it blocks routes like a rail, physics gives it a skirt down to the ground, and a truck that drives off it flies. A prop box inside a deck outline whose top is under the deck line is out of reach (`underDeck()`), and `blockingBoxes()` drops it, so the wing rests on its hull drums without them blocking the deck. `src/three/render/ship-decks.ts` draws the wing and flap models on their decks, with a skirt down to the ground under each rail and lip. The map stays one level on every deck. `heightAt` in `src/sim/terrain.ts` returns the deck height on a deck, and `groundAt` returns the ground under it. Rails on both long edges block routes and physics, so trucks get on only over the ends. A deck with `cut` removes the road's flattening in its gap, as Canyon Bridge does over its canyon. The Broken Wing deck has no `cut` and has a `skirt`: its physics rails reach down past the lowest ground under them, so a truck on the ground cannot drive in under the slab. On screen the skirt is the lattice and pylons of `wing_deck`, drawn by `buildWingDeck` in `src/three/render/sites.ts`.
- `src/sim/weather.ts` owns weather. `stormStrength` gives a storm's build-up and clearing over time from the turn it was born, and `stormDepth` multiplies it by the edge falloff. `weatherAt` is the settled weather at a place: the target for exposure, and the region heat that `src/sim/sun.ts` reads. A truck's saved `stormExposure` holds how far each storm has got into it, and `advanceExposure` steps it toward `stormDepth` by at most `1 / stormExposeTurns` a turn, at the end of `advanceWeather`. `weatherOn` turns that exposure into the effects every truck reads: sight, speed, aim spread, wear and the dust trail. `stormShare` gives the player's largest share, which sets the screen tint and the wind loop. The storm haze reads `stormStrength`, and only eases its steps between frames. `src/sim/sun.ts` owns time of day and sun heat, and `src/sim/engine-heat.ts` the player's engine heat. `src/sim/vision.ts` owns sight and fog of war, and `src/sim/locations.ts` discovery.
