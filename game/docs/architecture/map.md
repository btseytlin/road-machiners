# Map and world

- The map is baked offline, never built in the game. `src/mapgen/bake.ts` runs fixed layers over one draft: base relief, geology, road and site finish, the old world, the new world, territories, ground types and boulders.
- `src/mapgen/newworld.ts` grows scrub from moist ground, fills small basins with dirty water or toxic pools, and places shack camps with fences and junk, and car wrecks, with numbers in `NEW_WORLD`.
- `src/mapgen/oldworld.ts` places ruined settlements, overlook and bend buildings, old asphalt roads, old highways with broken bridges over deep gorges, power lines, billboards, tank hulks and dead fields, with numbers in `OLD_WORLD` in `src/data/terrain.ts`.
- `src/mapgen/geology.ts` holds the rain, slump, wind and dune rules, with numbers in `GEOLOGY` in `src/data/terrain.ts`.
- A territory is a location of kind `territory` in `REGION.locations`: no edge, gates, pads or `site` obstacle, no flattening and no hardpan, so trucks drive in over the natural ground. Its shape is a circle of `radius` round `pos`, or the polygon `outline` when one is set. Old Orchard's outline follows its basin; for an outline, `radius` is its bounding radius, a reach and never an edge. Its rules live in `TERRITORIES` in `src/data/territory.ts`: a seed offset, the `spine` the territory lies along, an optional `hull` (sections, walls and bays), an optional `farm`, spot and debris rules, the reactor and the hazard. The Fallen Sun has a hull and its crash line as the spine. Old Orchard has a farm and its old road as the spine.
- `siteGap(site, pos)` in `src/sim/sites.ts` owns every location's edge: tiles from pos to the edge, negative inside, from the outline or the circle. `siteEdgeCrossings()` gives where a segment crosses it. Membership, entries, discovery, the old-world clearance, the old-road router, road paint and grading, nav and the farm bake all call these two, and nothing else compares a distance with a location's radius. `groundDiscs()` in `src/render/groundPaint.ts` paints no disc under an outlined location.
- `src/sim/territory.ts` answers every territory question for the sim: membership, spots, entries, hunting grounds and hazard zones. It is also the one owner of hull deck geometry (`hullDecks`, `deckAlongAt`, `deckPlane`, `ribPoses`, `bayPoints`), so the bake and the render agree on every deck. `src/sim/hazard.ts` drains driver health inside a zone, and the nav layer blocks zones for routes only.
- `src/mapgen/territory.ts` fills each territory with its own rng, drawn from the map seed and the offset `TERRITORY_SEED_OFFSET + rules.seed`, so adding a territory shifts no other's draws. It stamps the hull decks into the corner heights first, then places the reactor, the ribs, the deck bays and the walls, then lays out the farm, then draws field spots and debris in the band along the spine. Spots come before debris, so debris never boxes one in. Drawn props keep off New World roads, cliffs, and a farm's old road, pads and tracks.
  - A deck is ground, not a second height source. The stamp sets each corner on a deck to the higher of the ground and the deck plane, which climbs from the ground at the deck's low end. So physics, nav, sight and slope rules treat a deck like a hill, and its steep sides and high end are cliffs with no new code.
  - Deck tiles get the `BUILT_HULL` mark in `d.built`. `MARKED_TYPES` in `src/mapgen/bake.ts` turns it into the `hull` ground type, so the ground layer never fills a deck with mud or salt.
  - `src/three/render/hull-decks.ts` lays a plate mesh on each deck from the same geometry and the baked heights. It is decoration only.
- `fillFarm()` in `src/mapgen/farm.ts` lays out a territory's `FarmRules`. Every position is authored in the road's frame: `onOrchardRoad(s, c)` in `src/data/region.ts` turns tiles `s` along the road toward its north end and `c` across it toward map west into an offset from the centre. `ORCHARD_HEADING` sets the road's heading so it shows at the concept image's 25° screen angle from the fixed camera. Building turns are relative to that heading. The authored skeleton is the roads, pads, canals, building poses, run lines and blocks; everything on it is jittered, broken or scattered from the territory rng. The steps run in order, and each mark takes only a tile no earlier mark took:
  - `roads` are marked in list order: an `oldRoad` as asphalt and a `track` as `BUILT_TRACK` (the `track` ground: hardpan to drive, painted paler so roads show). The first five are the main roads, and each ends within a tile of the outline, so it leaves the farm. The spur road in `REGION` ends at the old road's south end.
  - Pads are marked as `BUILT_PAD` (the `concrete` ground: drives like asphalt, painted pale grey) and `canals` as `BUILT_CANAL` (the `canal` ground: drives like dirty water, painted blue-grey).
  - Each building pose becomes a loot spot, turned by up to its group's `turnJitter` and shifted by up to `shift`. `spotTableAt()` in `src/sim/territory.ts` finds its table by its look, so one look has one table.
  - Runs of segment props (fences, barriers, sandbags) go along their polylines. A `broken` share of segments is dropped and each left segment turns and shifts by its `jitter`. A segment that touches a road, track, canal or pad, reaches into a building or crosses another segment is dropped.
  - Every block marks its field, turned `turn` off the road's frame. Then `clutter` scatters loose pieces round the buildings of the looks each rule names, from the debris gap to `reach` tiles past the building, off roads, marks and fields.
  - Each block gets trees on a grid in its row direction, with jitter, missing points and random turns, then `strays` single trees stand just past the block edges, up to `groves.maxTrees`. Trees keep off the roads, marks, runs, clutter and every building's parking gap.
  - No three run or clutter pieces of one look stand in a ruled row, within 0.1 tile of one line and 0.02 rad of one turn: a run segment that would make one turns a little more or drops, and a clutter draw that would is redrawn.
  - After the farm, drawn spots keep the spot gap from the buildings and the debris gap from the trees and runs. Old Orchard draws none: its army caches are authored with its buildings, since its groves leave no open band. Hunting grounds lie in the `grounds` band beside the spine, inside the outline.
- The farm fails loudly on authored parts and drops decoration. A building, pad, road point, canal point, run point or block corner off the outline, on a cliff or on a New World road throws. So does a building on a farm road unless its pose sets `shoulder`, or on a canal. A road's outer end may lie up to a tile past the edge. Trees, strays, run segments and clutter on bad ground are dropped, but a block whose ground rejects more than 30% of the trees it plans (its grid points not left empty) throws. Move an authored position onto good ground and note the old and new `(s, c)` in its comment, never loosen a check.
- Baked props stay out of saves and are rebuilt from the map file on load. `decodeMap()` in `src/sim/terrain.ts` reads the file. Boot fetches it in `src/three/main.ts`, and tests use `TEST_MAP` from `src/test/map.ts`.
- Broken Wing's hoop is a baked prop, `shipWing`, placed first by `shipWing()` in `oldworld.ts` from `TERRAIN.features.wing`. It is the wing's torn root, bent up and over the road. Its feet block like any ruin. Its boxes over the road start at least `truckClearance` + 1 m up, so physics, nav and sight leave the road under it open like a canopy. `BROKEN_WING` in `src/data/region.ts` places everything at Broken Wing: the straight road (map yaw 0), the hoop, the deck ends, the two sand mounds (`TERRAIN.features.mounds`, bowls with a negative depth, so road grading turns them into ramps), the crash trench (`TERRAIN.features.trench`, a channel) and the site.
- `src/sim/bridge.ts` holds the decks as a list, `DECKS`: Canyon Bridge and the Broken Wing deck. The map stays one level on every deck. `heightAt` in `src/sim/terrain.ts` returns the deck height on a deck, and `groundAt` returns the ground under it. Rails on both long edges block routes and physics, so trucks get on only over the ends. A deck with `cut` removes the road's flattening in its gap, as Canyon Bridge does over its canyon. The Broken Wing deck has no `cut` and has a `skirt`: its physics rails reach down past the lowest ground under them, so a truck on the ground cannot drive in under the slab. On screen the skirt is the lattice and pylons of `wing_deck`, drawn by `buildWingDeck` in `src/three/render/sites.ts`.
- `src/sim/weather.ts` answers `weatherAt`, which every weather effect reads. Its `stormStrength` owns a storm's build-up and clearing over time from the turn it was born. The storm haze, screen tint and wind loop read it too, and the haze only eases its steps between frames. `src/sim/sun.ts` owns time of day and sun heat, and `src/sim/engine-heat.ts` the player's engine heat. `src/sim/vision.ts` owns sight and fog of war, and `src/sim/locations.ts` discovery.
